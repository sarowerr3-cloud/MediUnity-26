1. Executive Summary

MediUnity is a full-stack, multi-portal healthcare platform that connects patients, doctors, hospitals, diagnostic centers, and pharmacies under a single unified system. The platform handles the entire lifecycle of healthcare delivery — from discovering a doctor, booking an appointment, paying online, receiving a video consultation, getting a digital prescription, ordering medicines, and tracking the entire process with SMS reminders.

The platform consists of 4 independently runnable applications:

ApplicationPortPurpose
Backend API4000REST API server handling all business logic, authentication, and data
Frontend (Patient + Doctor)5173Patient & Doctor-facing web app
Admin Panel5174Administrative dashboard for platform management
Launch ScriptN/ALAUNCH_MEDI_UNITY.bat starts all services simultaneously

7.3 Data Encryption at Rest

  • Algorithm: AES-256-GCM (authenticated encryption)
  • Encrypted Fields: Patient email, phone, NID, birth cert number, emergency contacts, medical history notes
  • Blind Indexing: SHA-256 hashed email/phone for searchability without decryption
  • Doctor Fields: Email encrypted; password bcrypt-hashed

8. Patient Portal — Features

8.1 Doctor Discovery & Booking

  • Browse, search, and filter doctors by specialty, location, availability
  • Full doctor profile with ratings, reviews, BMDC badge, chambers, timeslots
  • Book: select date/time/type → pay online (aamarPay/Stripe) or Cash → receive serial number
  • Video Consultation: Embedded Jitsi Meet for real-time calls

8.2 Health Management

  • Health Tracker: Log mood, symptoms, blood pressure, weight
  • Symptom Checker: Interactive checker with voice recognition support → recommends specialties
  • Recovery Journal: Private daily entries for tracking wellbeing
  • My Health Dashboard: Aggregated health data, prescriptions, medical files

8.3 Community & Knowledge

  • Forum: Post health questions anonymously, get doctor answers
  • Articles: Curated health articles by platform doctors
  • Social Feed: Posts with likes, comments, media sharing

8.4 Service Booking

  • Hospitals: Browse, view services, book beds and consultations
  • Diagnostics: View test catalogs with prices/prep instructions, book tests
  • Pharmacies: Browse inventory with prices, place orders

8.5 Other Features

  • Messages: Direct messaging with doctors
  • Tracking: Track any booking by serial number — works without login
  • Notifications: Real-time SSE + 24hr SMS reminders
  • Identity Verification: NID/birth certificate upload → OCR scanning → admin review
  • Medical File Locker: Secure document storage on Cloudinary
  • Internationalization: English & Bangla (বাংলা) via i18next
  • PWA: Offline caching, background sync, installable app

9. Doctor Portal — Features

  • Dashboard: Today's appointments, patient queue (Scheduled → CheckedIn → InConsultation → Completed), revenue analytics
  • Analytics Tab: Revenue trends, patient volume, consultation type breakdown, commission tracking
  • Schedule Manager: Set timeslots, recurring slots, blocked periods, blackouts, max patients/day, per-slot chambers
  • Appointment List: Accept/cancel/reschedule, start video calls, write prescriptions, refer patients, schedule follow-ups
  • Profile Editor: Update all professional information and profile image
  • Gamification: Reputation points, BMDC verified badge, follower count
